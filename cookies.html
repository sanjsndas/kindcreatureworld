🍪 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or smartphone) when you visit websites. They help us remember your actions and preferences (like login details, language, font size, and pet care filters) so you don’t have to re-enter them on every visit. At Kind Creature World, we use cookies to make your experience smoother, more personal, and secure—just like a warm blanket for your digital journey.

🐕 Transparency first: We never use cookies to collect sensitive personal data without your explicit consent. Your trust is our guiding star.

📌 2. How We Use Cookies

We utilize cookies for several essential and optional purposes:

  • Essential functionality: Enable core features like secure account access, donation forms, and pet health toolkit.
  • Performance & analytics: Understand how visitors interact with our grey & yellow pages, which adoption stories are most loved, and improve site speed.
  • Preferences & personalization: Remember your language, preferred pet content (cats, dogs, exotic pets), and accessibility settings.
  • Security: Help detect fraudulent activity and protect our community from bots or abuse.

We do not use cookies to serve cross-site behavioral advertising based on sensitive health or pet data. Any marketing-related tracking is strictly opt-in.

🌐 4. Third-Party Cookies & Partners

Certain features on our website rely on trusted third-party services that may place cookies. These include:

  • Payment processors: Stripe / PayPal — cookies to enable secure checkout (strictly functional).
  • Customer support widgets: Help Scout or similar — improve chat experience.
  • Analytics partners: Self-hosted Plausible or anonymized Google Analytics 4 (IP masking enabled, no data sharing).

We require all third parties to respect data protection laws and sign data processing agreements. You can block third-party cookies via browser settings, but it may affect certain features (e.g., live chat).

⚙️ 5. How to Manage & Delete Cookies

You have full authority over cookies. You can:

  • Adjust your preferences using the Cookie Consent Manager below (real-time control).
  • Change browser settings: most browsers allow you to block all cookies, delete existing ones, or alert you before a cookie is stored.
  • Opt-out of analytics cookies by toggling the "Analytics" option in our manager — this will set a “do-not-track” preference.

Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect core website functions such as forms, adoption applications, and donation processing.
