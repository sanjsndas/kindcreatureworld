Privacy Policy

Your trust is our priority. At Kind Creature World, we protect your personal data as fiercely as we care for your furry, feathered, and scaled companions.

📅 Last Updated: June 4, 2026 • Effective from June 11, 2026

🐕 1. Information We Collect

Kind Creature World respects your privacy. We collect information to provide better pet care services, improve our website, and communicate with our compassionate community. The types of data we gather include:

  • Personal identifiers: Name, email address, phone number, mailing address (when you contact us, sign up for newsletters, adopt, or request vet assistance).
  • Pet-related details: Pet type, breed, age, health concerns (only if voluntarily shared via forms, consultations, or support tickets).
  • Payment & transaction data: If you purchase our grey & yellow product line or donate, we collect billing information via secure PCI-compliant gateways — we never store full card details.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, pages visited, and referral source (via analytics tools).
  • Communication history: Records of emails, chat messages, helpline calls for service quality and training.
✨ Our promise: We never sell your personal information to third parties. Your data fuels kindness, not profit extraction.

📋 2. How We Use Your Information

Your data helps us build a safer, more loving world for pets and their humans. Typical uses include:

  • Responding to contact form inquiries, emergency support requests, or partnership proposals.
  • Processing donations, orders, or event registrations for adoption fairs & educational workshops.
  • Sending updates about pet health tips, product recalls, or “Yellow Paw” community events (only if you opt-in).
  • Improving website functionality, personalizing user experience, and preventing fraud.
  • Conducting internal research about pet wellness trends (data aggregated and anonymized).

We may also use your email to send important service announcements (e.g., security updates, changes to this policy). You can unsubscribe from marketing messages anytime.

🤝 3. Sharing & Disclosure

We only share your personal information in limited circumstances and with strict safeguards:

  • Service providers: Trusted partners who help operate our website, process payments, send emails, or manage customer support. They are bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Veterinary & emergency partners: When you use our 24/7 helpline, necessary health data may be shared with licensed vet professionals to provide accurate triage.
  • Legal compliance: If required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of Kind Creature World, users, or animals.
  • Business transitions: In case of merger or acquisition, your data will be transferred under the same privacy commitments.

We never rent, sell, or trade your personal data for marketing purposes. Any third-party access is strictly controlled.

🍪 4. Cookies & Similar Technologies

Our website uses cookies and tracking pixels to enhance your experience and analyze traffic. Cookies help remember your preferences (e.g., language, pet type filters).

  • Essential cookies: Required for navigation and secure forms.
  • Analytics cookies: We use first-party analytics (like privacy-focused Plausible or anonymized Google Analytics settings) to understand page performance — always without identifying individual visitors.
  • Preference cookies: Remember your choices such as “hide emergency banner” or font size.

You can manage cookie preferences via browser settings. However, disabling essential cookies may affect form submission. For full details, see our Cookie Policy.

🔒 5. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard safeguards: encryption (TLS 1.3), restricted access databases, routine vulnerability scans, and employee training. Your information is stored on secure servers with 24/7 monitoring.

Retention periods: We keep personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. For example:

  • Contact form inquiries: 3 years after last interaction.
  • Transaction records: 7 years for tax/accounting compliance.
  • Pet health consultation notes: anonymized after 2 years.

If you request deletion, we will erase or de-identify your data unless retention is required by law.

📌 6. Your Privacy Rights (CCPA, GDPR & more)

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to know & access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to correction: Update inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to deletion: Ask us to delete your personal information, subject to exceptions.
  • Right to opt-out of “sale/sharing”: We do not sell data, but you may still control targeted ads via cookie preferences.
  • Right to data portability: Receive a machine-readable copy of your data.
  • Right to withdraw consent: For any processing based on consent (e.g., marketing emails).

To exercise your rights, please contact us via the email or phone number in Section 10. We will respond within 30 days and never discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights.

🧸 7. Children’s Privacy

Kind Creature World is a family-friendly platform, but we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. If you believe your child has provided us with data without consent, please contact us immediately. For teen users (13-18), we encourage involvement of a parent or guardian when submitting contact forms or participating in events.

🌍 8. International Data Transfers

Our headquarters are in the United States, but we serve pet families worldwide. If you access our website from outside the US, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the US or other countries. We ensure appropriate safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent) are in place for data transfers from the EEA, UK, or Switzerland. By using our services, you acknowledge such transfers when necessary.

📢 9. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements, our data practices, or operational needs. The “Last Updated” date at the top of this page indicates when the latest version was published. For material changes, we will notify you via email (if you have an account) or through a prominent notice on our website. Your continued use of the website after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
